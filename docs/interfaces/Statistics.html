    Interface Statistics

    Statistics separate describing the samples from inferring about their mean. Location and dispersion use the samples' units (ms for latency, ops/s for throughput), except variance (squared units). Relative margin of error is a percentage; counts and critical values are dimensionless.

    mean, the percentiles, min and max locate the individual samples; variance, sd, aad and mad describe how the samples spread. These are properties of the collected samples, not guarantees about future runs. Percentiles interpolate linearly between order statistics; on small samples the upper percentiles interpolate between the few largest observations, so they are unstable estimates of the tail.

    sem, moe and rme concern the mean of the sampled process. With at least two samples, moe is the half-width of a nominal two-sided 95% confidence interval for the mean, computed with the exact Student's t critical value Statistics.critical for Statistics.df degrees of freedom; with a single sample, moe is 0 and does not define a meaningful confidence interval. Nominal coverage assumes independent samples from a stable regime and an approximately normal sample mean; it can be optimistic under strong skew or regime shifts, especially on small samples. It covers only the sampled process — run-to-run and process-to-process variation is not included.
